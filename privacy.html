ProgressScape Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

ProgressScape is a free Old School RuneScape progression tracker at progressscape.net, plus an optional RuneLite plugin that syncs your in-game progress to it. This policy covers both, and explains what is collected, what is stored only on your own device, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

ProgressScape is a fan-made project run by one person. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jagex Ltd.

Using the website without the plugin

You do not need an account, and you do not need the plugin. If you just tick things off on the site, that progress is saved in your own browser's local storage and is never sent anywhere. It includes things like which entries you have completed, your skill levels, boss kill counts, collection log ticks, Combat Achievement ticks, custom plans and notes, your last looked-up RSN, and your light/dark preference.

Because it lives in your browser, clearing your site data or switching device or browser will lose it. The Export option on the Progression page saves a file you can import elsewhere.

What the RuneLite plugin sends

If you install the plugin, it uploads the following to the ProgressScape database. Most of it is sent automatically when you log in and log out; the collection log is only sent when you press the sync button with your collection log open in game.

  • Your OSRS account username
  • Your account type (Normal, Ironman, Hardcore Ironman, Ultimate Ironman, Group Ironman)
  • Quest completion status for all quests
  • Achievement Diary completion status (Easy, Medium, Hard, Elite) for all diary regions
  • Combat Achievement tasks you have completed
  • Boss kill counts, taken from the public OSRS hiscores and from in-game kill count chat messages
  • Collection log items obtained, only when you manually trigger a sync

What is never collected

  • Bank contents
  • Grand Exchange history
  • Your password, email address or any payment information
  • Chat logs, private messages, or anything about other players
  • Your real name or any personally identifying information beyond your OSRS username

Your progress is public by username

This is the part worth reading twice. ProgressScape works by letting anyone type in an RSN and see that account's progression, so anything the plugin uploads is readable by anyone who knows or guesses your username. That is the point of the tracker, but it does mean you should not install the plugin if you would rather your progress stayed private. If you change your mind, you can have your data deleted using the contact details below.

Analytics and cookies

The site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to count visits and to see which features get used, so time goes into the parts people actually use. This sets cookies in your browser and sends Google your IP address, approximate location, device and browser type, and the pages you visit.

It also records anonymous events such as a lookup being performed, an entry being ticked off, or a tool being opened. These deliberately do not include your RSN or any other identifying detail — the record is that a lookup happened and whether plugin data was found, never whose account it was for.

If you would rather not be counted, a browser ad blocker or tracking blocker will stop it, and the site is built to work normally when it is blocked.

Other services the site contacts

  • Supabase — hosts the database your progression is stored in and read back from.
  • Cloudflare Workers — a small proxy used to fetch the official OSRS hiscores. Your RSN is passed through it to perform the lookup.
  • prices.runescape.wiki — live Grand Exchange prices. No personal data is sent.
  • oldschool.runescape.wiki — item and boss images.
  • Google Fonts — the fonts the site is set in.
  • Google Analytics — as described above.

Any service your browser contacts can see your IP address as part of making the request. Each of these has its own privacy policy.

Where data is stored

Uploaded progression data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database in the Asia-Pacific (Sydney, Australia) region, against your OSRS username only. No passwords or payment information are ever collected or stored, because there are no accounts and nothing is for sale.

How data is used

Solely to power the tracker at progressscape.net — so you, and anyone you share your username with, can see your progression. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with anyone for commercial purposes.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded data is kept for as long as you use the plugin. To have all data for your account removed, email the address below with your OSRS username. Deletion will be completed within 7 days. Uninstalling the plugin stops any further uploads, but does not by itself remove what has already been sent — ask for deletion if you want it gone.

Progress stored only in your browser is yours to clear at any time through your browser's site data settings.

Changes to this policy

If what is collected changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
